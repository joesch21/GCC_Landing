Gold Condor does not ask an autonomous agent to trust a marketing claim. It exposes the data, economics, authority and settlement conditions required for the agent to make its own decision.

Condor should be as capable of rejecting a bad economic opportunity as executing a good one.

GCC is a connected liquidity and settlement network exposed to several external market regimes. Condor converts that complexity into transparent, machine-readable economic intelligence for autonomous agents.

Historical evidence is not a live opportunity signal. Current gauges remain unavailable when live inputs or required score components are missing.
